Welcome to CSE at the University of Barishal! Here, we combine rigorous academics with hands-on experience, guiding students to become skilled professionals and innovators. Our faculty, research initiatives, and vibrant student community work together to advance knowledge and make a meaningful impact on society.

About The Department

The Computer Science and Engineering (CSE) department teaches the basics of computing, programming, and problem-solving. Students learn coding, algorithms, databases, and computer systems.

The department also covers modern topics like Artificial Intelligence and Cybersecurity. Practical labs and projects help students improve technical skills. Graduates can work in software development, data analysis, or IT fields. CSE is a fast-growing area that prepares students for technology-driven careers.
